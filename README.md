# T-Out

Days out for Theo. Pick somewhere that fits today: raining or dry, free or paid, close or worth the drive.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It's one self-contained page.

## Using it

- **Pick** chooses a place that fits the filters. Places you haven't been come up more often, places from your own list beat ideas, and recent picks rarely repeat.
- **✓** logs that you went today.
- **Chips** (Raining, Free, Close by…) are quick filters. The slider icon opens the full set: weather, cost, travel time, mood, kind, source.
- **List** shows everything grouped by kind. Tap a place to fix its tags, add a note, or remove it.
- **+** adds places. Paste names one per line, or drop in Google's `Want to go.csv` (takeout.google.com, tick **Saved**). Tags are guessed, and an orange dot means check them.

Ideas around Belfast are built in (`IDEAS` in `index.html`). Travel times are rough driving times from the city centre. Everything is saved in the browser (`localStorage`). Use **+ → Save a backup** to keep a copy.
